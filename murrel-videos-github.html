Murrel Fish Farming Tips

  • Water Temperature: 26°C – 32°C promotes optimal metabolic growth.
  • Cannibalism Control: Grade fingerlings every 10-14 days by size to prevent mortality.
  • Anti-Jump Covering: Keep tanks covered; Murrels are agile jumpers, especially during night rains.
  • High Nutritional Value: Rich in natural albumin and amino acids for rapid healing.
